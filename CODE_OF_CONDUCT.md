# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit others' work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private manuscripts, personal data or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private security advisory channel or another maintainer-approved private channel. Do not create a public issue containing personal or sensitive information.
